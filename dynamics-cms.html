CMS & Promotions

Control the player-facing experience without a development queue: content, translations, category names, banners, and promotional presentation — managed from a back office that stays practical across desktop and mobile, so the brand stays relevant across markets and campaigns.
Core CMS Scope
● Lobby content and structure — dynamic management (see Lobby)
● Translations and dynamic naming — categories and other UI elements
● Banners and promotional content — coordinated with active campaigns
● Promotional presentation — matched to campaign mechanics and eligibility
● Localized labels and navigation per market
● Instant changes from a mobile-compatible back office
Mobile-Compatible Back Office

Make instant website changes from a mobile-compatible operational interface:

Live events Campaign launches Urgent content changes Seasonal updates Market-specific edits

Especially valuable when a desktop-only workflow would slow the response — teams act on live information without waiting to return to a workstation.

Brand Management
From the Dynamics product deck

A one-stop shop for configuring a new brand — fast, intuitive setup covering:

● Countries, currencies, languages, domains — plus player, captcha, and verification settings
● Centralized sportsbook layout, widget, and wallet configuration
● Payment settings in one place — balance, deposit, withdrawal — and taxes for deposits and withdrawals
● Notification service, operator limitations, location services, conversion settings, and standards
● SEO settings — SEO, social network, and schema-markup configuration
● Third-party services with suggested defaults (Zendesk, Intercom, Hotjar, Google Analytics, Google Search Console) plus custom scripts
UI Customization
From the Dynamics product deck

Highly flexible and customer-focused — a custom, unique approach per brand:

● Multiple templates, with color and shape changes across the UI
● Brand elements managed on the website — logo, colors, fonts, even icon styles
● Customizable navigation, content display, payment options, and product coverage
● Skinning extends to system notifications, the cashier module, and bonus management
● A multilingual, fully responsive site — updated dynamically as the business needs change

Promotions, engagement & communication
Banner & Promotional Content
Create and manage banners and promotional content that support Bonus Engine, referral, cashback, CRM, and gamification campaigns. Centralized content control helps ensure the offer presented in the lobby matches the actual campaign mechanics and eligibility.
Translation & Dynamic Naming
Manage translations and dynamically name categories and other UI elements. Local teams adapt terminology, campaign messaging, and navigation to the market — without maintaining disconnected versions of the product.
Performance & Operational Insight
Use real-time metrics and reports to review player activity, game performance, and revenue indicators — and adjust content placement and promotional emphasis based on live business information instead of static assumptions.
Built-In Player Communication
Native Email, SMS, Push, and Inbox communications with multilingual templates, segmented distribution, scheduling, and a real-time device preview before sending. Push adds audience selection (predefined groups or all players including guests), language- and device-based composition, and deep linking so players land exactly where the action is.
